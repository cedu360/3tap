Protected payment

You pay in the app. The money only goes out when the job is done.

Accepted the price? Pay by Pix inside the order. The amount is held and released to the professional when you confirm completion. If something goes wrong, Report a problem holds the release until the team reviews it.

  1. Only the agreed price. Customers pay no extra fee. The platform fee comes out of the professional's share, and they see it before sending the price.
  2. A clear deadline to pay. 30 minutes for immediate jobs, 24 hours for scheduled ones. Missed it? The order doesn't count as paid and the professional's slot is freed.
  3. No-surprise cancellation. Clear rules for each situation: free until the professional heads out, and a full refund when they are the one who backs out.
  4. Every movement recorded. Payment checked directly with the financial institution, never by a receipt, with a history you can look up.

Payments processed by an institution authorized by Brazil's Central Bank. 3Tap does not see or store your bank details.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Didn't find your answer? Open the app and talk to the Help Center from your profile.

Does 3Tap charge customers anything?

No. Creating an account is free, and customers pay only the price agreed with the professional, with no extra fee. The platform fee is deducted from what the independent professional receives, and they see it before sending the price. Businesses have their own plans, starting with the free Starter plan.

How does payment work?

You accept the price and pay in the app, by Pix, inside the order. The money is held until you confirm the job is done. Then it's released to the professional. Payment is processed by an institution authorized by Brazil's Central Bank.

What if something goes wrong with the job?

Tap Report a problem in the order. The money stays on hold until the 3Tap team reviews the chat and history. The team can release the amount, refund it all or refund part of it.

Can I cancel?

Yes, and the rules show up in the order before you decide. For immediate jobs, cancelling is free until the professional is “on the way”; for scheduled ones, up to 24 hours before. If the professional backs out before starting, you get everything back.

I don't know which professional to call. Now what?

Write, speak or send a photo of the problem. 3Tap suggests the trade and shows who serves your area, with free time on the date you asked for. You can change the trade if you prefer.

What is “I need help now!”?

A path just for urgent needs: towing, locksmith, electrician, plumber, mechanic, refrigeration. 3Tap shows only who is available at that moment, ordered by arrival time, and sends the request straight to the professional. In a life-threatening emergency, call 192, 193 or 190 (Brazil): 3Tap does not replace these services.

How does the video inspection work?

When the professional asks, a Record video button appears in the chat. You record a short video of the problem, they watch it and send a fixed price. You don't both need to be free at the same time. A live video call is still an option.

How do I know if a professional is trustworthy?

Look at the rating, the verified reviews (only from people who hired and paid in the app), the share of completed jobs, response time and portfolio. Documents and qualifications are shown as submitted by the professional: 3Tap does not present them as verified.

Do I have to give out my phone number?

No. Customer and professional talk through 3Tap's own chat and voice and video calls, and nobody's number is shown.

Can businesses join 3Tap too?

Yes. Garages, schools, salons, courses and other businesses get a business page and show up in search next to independent professionals. They start on the free Starter plan and can choose a monthly plan with more features.

Does 3Tap work in my city?

3Tap started in Itabirito (MG, Brazil) and works from your location, in any city. Where there are still few professionals, the app widens the range on its own, looks in nearby cities and lets you know when someone suitable shows up.

How do I work through 3Tap?

Create your account, choose Work and add your services, prices, service area and availability. That's it: you already show up for customers near you. If you already have a customer account, just turn on professional mode in Profile.

How do referrals work?

When someone joins 3Tap through your referral, the commission the app would earn on that person's jobs in their first month goes to you. After the first month, it goes back to normal.
